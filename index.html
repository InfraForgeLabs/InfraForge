InfraForge

Open-Source Infrastructure Automation Platform

InfraForge is an open-source infrastructure automation and DevSecOps platform developed by InfraForge Labs. It unifies infrastructure provisioning, CI/CD, security, and GitOps workflows — while remaining fully local and user-controlled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is InfraForge free?

Yes. InfraForge is 100% free and open-source under the MIT License.

Does InfraForge deploy infrastructure automatically?

InfraForge generates templates locally. Actual deployment requires explicit user confirmation.

Is InfraForge cloud-dependent?

No. InfraForge is local-first and does not require cloud access to operate.

When does InfraForge require internet access?

Internet access is used only for pulling or updating infrastructure templates from upstream sources. All generation and execution happen locally on your machine.

Privacy & Data

Does InfraForge collect any data?

No. InfraForge does not collect, track, or transmit user data.

Does InfraForge require accounts or login?

No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics. Everything runs locally.

Is any data sent to InfraForge Labs?

No. InfraForge never sends user data to InfraForge Labs or third parties.
