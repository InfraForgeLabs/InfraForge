InfraForge

Open-Source Infrastructure Automation Platform

InfraForge is an open-source infrastructure automation and DevSecOps platform developed by InfraForge Labs. It unifies infrastructure provisioning, CI/CD, security, and GitOps workflows — while remaining fully local and user-controlled.
