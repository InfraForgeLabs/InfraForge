# 💼 InfraForge BUSINESS_MODEL.md

## 🌍 Overview
InfraForge operates on a **sustainable open-source model** focused on transparency, accessibility, and community empowerment. It provides advanced DevSecOps automation tools that remain **free, local, and open forever**, with optional self-hostable extensions for enterprise scalability.

---

## 💡 Core Business Model

### 1. **Open Core Foundation**
- Core framework (scripts, CLIs, templates) remains open-source under MIT License.
- No paid features are required for functional use.
- Focused on developer freedom and educational access.

### 2. **Optional Enterprise Tier (Post-Funding)**
- Introduce self-hostable enterprise modules: audit dashboards, policy compliance engines, and runtime analytics.
- All enterprise tools remain deployable on private infrastructure — **no SaaS lock-in**.

### 3. **Community Sponsorships & Donations**
- Funded through **GitHub Sponsors**, **Buy Me a Coffee**, and open education partnerships.
- Sponsorship tiers may offer early access, certifications, or recognition badges.

### 4. **Collaborative Growth**
- Partner with educational institutions, DevOps bootcamps, and industry contributors.
- Develop community-driven IaC packs, security rules, and observability templates.

---

## 📈 Growth Strategy
- Establish **InfraForgeLabs** as the parent innovation hub.
- Expand through consistent roadmap delivery and transparent changelogs.
- Reinforce brand trust via practical documentation and contributor engagement.
- Grow ecosystem contributions via open RFCs, mentorship, and challenges.

---

## 💰 Funding Model
- Transparent funding via **GitHub Sponsors** and **OpenCollective**.
- Reinvestment in documentation, contributor rewards, and infrastructure testing.
- Optional enterprise support and private consulting (post v6.5.0).

---

## 🧭 Long-Term Sustainability
InfraForge’s business direction will **never compromise its free-core philosophy**.  
It will grow through openness, community collaboration, and optional professional services — ensuring that the automation framework remains **Free · Local · Open · Forever.**
